Hotels & Resorts

The challenge

Every guest showers in the same two morning hours, and the pool, kitchen and laundry need hot water too.

What we install

Central heat pump plants with large storage tanks and circulation pumps, sized for full occupancy, so the last guest gets the same hot shower as the first.

  • Deron air to water heat pumps
  • HAASE energy storage tanks
  • Wilo circulation pumps
  • Pool heat pumps

Hospitals & Clinics

The challenge

Wards, operating rooms, kitchens and laundries need hot water around the clock, at safe and steady temperatures.

What we install

Heat pumps backed up by electric heaters, hygienic storage tanks that keep bacteria out, and mixing valves, so supply never stops and never scalds.

  • Heat pumps
  • Rotex Sanicube hygienic tanks
  • Champion storage tanks
  • Maintenance plans
Trusted by

Condos & Residential Towers

The challenge

Developers need units that fit small bathrooms, work on every floor's water pressure and are easy for owners to look after.

What we install

Instant or storage heaters for each unit, chosen for the floor plan and pressure, or one central system for the whole tower.

  • Aquapower instant heaters
  • Gratek Aquarius with pump
  • Rheem storage heaters
  • PPR pipes & fittings

Restaurants, Gyms & Offices

The challenge

Kitchens need hot water for cleaning at peak hours. Gyms need dozens of showers running at once after classes.

What we install

Systems sized to your busiest hour: storage heaters or heat pumps with tanks, plus exhaust fans for kitchens and changing rooms.

  • Everhot storage heaters
  • 5 Star integral heat pumps
  • Aquapower Q9M instant heaters
  • Katen exhaust fans

How it works

One System, Four Parts

A typical central system for a hotel or hospital. Tap any part to see the equipment we use.

Swipe to see the whole system.

Hot waterSolar heatReturn to be reheated
01 · HEATHeat pumps

Move heat from the air into the water, using about a quarter of the power of electric heaters.

02 · STOREStorage tank

Holds enough hot water for your busiest hour, ready before the morning rush.

03 · MOVECirculation pump

Keeps hot water moving in a loop, so every tap on every floor runs hot in seconds.

04 · RETURNReturn loop

Cooled water flows back to the tank to be reheated. Nothing is wasted down the drain while you wait.

How we work

From Survey to
Service, One Team

You deal with the same company from the first site visit to the tenth maintenance check.

  1. Site survey

    Free. Our sales engineers visit, look at the plant room, the roof and the pipes, and ask how the building is used.

  2. System design

    We size the heaters, tanks and pumps for your peak hour, and plan where everything goes.
