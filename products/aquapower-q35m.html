Why choose it

What You Get

Warranty: 5 years on the heating element, 1 year on parts.

Fast heating, copper tank

The 99.9% copper tank transfers heat quickly and stands up to years of use.

Works on low water pressure

It switches on at just 2 liters a minute, so it suits condos and upper floors with weak pressure.

No scalding, no overheating

Two thermostats cut the power if the water gets too hot, with automatic and manual reset.

Shuts itself off when something is wrong

A pressure switch turns the heater off if the water flow becomes irregular.

Splash-proof

Water and dust resistant casing, rated IP25, safe to mount in the shower area.

Built to last

Tested for over 3,000 hours of continuous use. It can last 30 years with clean water.

Compact

Small enough to mount beside the shower, or hide out of sight.
