Why choose it

What You Get

Every unit is genuine, installed by our own technicians and backed by our after-sales team.

Tough glass lining

A 2 mm acid-resistant blue glass lining resists abrasion, chemicals and knocks. Widely used for drinking water.

Rated for pressure

125 PSI as standard, 150 PSI available, and tested at 300 PSI.

Holds heat

50 mm of high-density fiberglass wool under an aluminum jacket.

Everything included

Magnesium anode rod, pressure and temperature relief valve, pressure and temperature gauges, manhole, drain, lifting lugs and three support legs.
