Overview

About This
Product

Aquaplast PP-R pipes and fittings carry hot water without rusting or scaling. They are made in Turkey by Formül Plastik, rated up to 95°C, and meet DIN 8077, DIN 8078 and EN ISO 15874.

Plain PP-R pipe comes in pressure classes PN 10 to PN 20, and composite pipe in PN 20 and PN 25, from 20 to 125 mm across. Elbows, couplings, adapters and valves come in the same sizes, so we can supply and install a complete hot water line from one maker.

How it works

Joints That
Can't Leak

PPR pipes are joined by heat, so pipe and fitting become a single piece. Scroll through it step by step, or tap a number.

  1. 01 · Step

    Heat both ends

    A fusion tool heats the pipe end and the fitting socket to about 260°C.

  2. 02 · Step

    Push them together

    The softened ends are pushed together and held for a few seconds.

  3. 03 · Step

    One solid piece

    As it cools, pipe and fitting fuse into one piece: no glue, no threads, nothing to work loose.

  4. 04 · Step

    Made for hot water

    PPR handles hot water temperatures and doesn't rust like steel pipe.

How to use it

How we install it

General guidance. Your technician shows you the exact settings at installation, and the manual has the details.

  1. Installed by our technicians

    Joints need a fusion welder and the right timing, so they are not a DIY job.

  2. Clipped with room to move

    Hot pipes expand, so runs are supported with clips and allowance for movement.

  3. Insulated where it counts

    Long hot runs are insulated to keep the heat in.

  4. Pressure-tested

    The finished system is pressure-tested before it is used.
