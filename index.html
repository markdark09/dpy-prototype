    How it works

    Inside a Storage
    Water Heater

    1. 01
      Cold water comes in

      Mains water enters at the bottom of the insulated tank.

    2. 02
      The elements heat it

      The upper element heats the top of the tank first, then the lower element heats the rest. Hot water rises.

    3. 03
      The thermostat holds it

      It switches the element on and off to keep the tank at the temperature you set.

    4. 04
      Hot water to every tap

      The outlet draws from the top, where the water is hottest, and sends it to your showers.

    Held at 50°C, ready at every tap, day and night. We'll size the right one for you.
