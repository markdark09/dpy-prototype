About us

25 Years of
Hot Water Done Right

DPY Mercantile Inc. and its home company, Gratek Smart Water Corp., supply, install and service water heaters for homes, hotels and hospitals across the Philippines.

  • 2001Founded
  • 5Branches
  • 12Brands

DPY Mercantile was set up in 2001 by Mr. Danilo P. Yap with one aim: to supply the most complete range of quality water heaters for homes, businesses and industry in the Philippines.

Partnerships with manufacturers from Germany, Australia and beyond give us the technology and the technical support to solve real engineering problems, and to recommend the most cost-effective system for each client.

What sets us apart is what happens after the sale. Our own technicians install every system, and our after-sales team keeps it running, from a single condo shower to a hospital's round-the-clock supply.

Everything we do follows world-class quality standards, and the same five values our team has worked by from the start.

Our story

From One Business
to Two Companies

  1. 2001DPY Mercantile begins

    Mr. Danilo P. Yap sets up DPY Mercantile in August 2001 to supply quality water heaters for homes, businesses and industry.

  2. 2011DPY Mercantile, Inc.

    On May 27, 2011 the business becomes a corporation, to grow into a leading supplier of water heaters, heat exchangers and after-sales service.

  3. GratekA company for homes

    Gratek Smart Water Corp. is formed to serve homeowners, condo developers and resorts with water heaters and hassle-free service.

  4. 2021Golden Globe awardee

    DPY is named an awardee of the Golden Globe Annual Awards for Business Excellence.

  5. TodayFive branches, twelve brands

    Sales and service from Manila, Boracay, Iloilo, Cebu and Davao, with partners from Germany, Australia and beyond.
